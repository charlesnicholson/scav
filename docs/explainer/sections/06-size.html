Phase 2

Sizing: coordinates, gaps, folds and packing

Phase 2 turns ranks and slots into rectangles, bottom-up from the deepest frames to the root.

The deepest frames are sized first, then their owner states, then those states' frames, up to the root; one final pass makes every coordinate root-absolute. So a leaf growing one unit resizes every ancestor.

Across the ranks: Brandes–Köpf

Each node's coordinate across the ranks comes from Brandes–Köpf (BK), a linear-time, balanced placement, run per connected component and fold piece with node_sep between neighbours. A labelled leg adds room for its label to its node's extent.

  1. Mark conflicts: an edge crossing a chain of bend nodes is marked, so long chains stay straight.
  2. Align four ways (layers forward or backward, each from its first or last node): each node joins its median neighbour into a block sharing one coordinate, unless the edge is marked or crosses an earlier alignment. Initials' edges align last, and a segment aligns where it meets a port, not at the composite's centre.
  3. Compact each block to its coordinate, following the 2020 erratum to the published algorithm.
  4. Balance: align the four results to the narrowest, and give each node the average of its two median values.

Then three alignment rules: states joined inside one column share a centre line; a state joined to a port is centred on it; an initial or final sits rank_sep from its mate, level with it.

Along the ranks: gaps

Each layer starts where the previous one ends, plus a gap:

gap = sep + max(label charge, lanes)

sep is rank_sep, or only the route clearance next to a layer holding just boundary nodes or seated pseudostates when no label is charged to the gap. The label charge comes from ordering. lanes is zero when fewer than two edges turn in the gap, otherwise one line height per turning edge. An edge whose ends overlap runs straight and does not turn.

Folding long rank runs

A long run of ranks can be folded: cut into pieces that are packed like rectangles, so a deep chain does not become one very wide strip.

  • The target width is the square root of the run's area scaled to the desired aspect ratio (DAR), at least the widest layer.
  • Cut where the running width passes the target, or at a fold pin, but never between a pseudostate or boundary node and the node it joins.
  • Keep the best of three by the scale measure (unless the frame's fold rule is Always or Never): unfolded, pieces packed, or pieces stacked. A fold whose pieces pack back into one row is not taken, and a frame running down never folds.
  • The charge on a gap a cut falls in, less node_sep, moves to the new piece's leading edge.
The scale measureSM = min(dar_num / (dar_den · w), 1 / h): how large the drawing could be scaled in a viewport of the desired aspect. Larger wins; ties go to the smaller area, then the closer aspect.

Packing

Packing places a frame's components, a fold's pieces, and a concurrent state's regions (sub_sep apart). States inside a frame are layered, never packed.

  • LR-rectpacking (Domrös et al.) gives each rectangle, in order, the first of four spots beside its predecessor that stays within the target width: Level, Right, Subrow, Row. Reading order is placement order.
  • Compaction (a Level 2 knob) lets each rectangle take another spot if the result is tighter and no extent grows.
  • Whitespace elimination grows rectangles to fill their rows; extents do not change.
  • The one-row box packer is also tried when the profile's trybox is set, and wins if it scales larger.

A packing aims at the profile's DAR (16:10 on readable) or, on some Level 2 rows, at the owner state's interior. A packing that would exceed COORD_MAX never displaces one that fits.

Bottom-up to the root

A composite's box wraps its packed submachines, its four bands and its loop room:

w = max(min_w, w_before + max(packed_w, loop_w) + w_after, kind_min_w) + 2·pad
h = max(h_before + packed_h + sep + loop_h + h_after, kind_min_h) + 2·pad

The h_* and w_* terms are the bands the application requested above, below, before and after the submachine area. The loop room, loop_w × loop_h, holds the state's internal self-transitions, one row each, below its submachines; sep is sub_sep when both are present, else 0. pad (128) is the interior ring. A bare pseudostate has none, so an arrow stops on its mark. A plain state is at least 896 × 640 on readable; pseudostate minimums are in the model.

After routing, the chart rectangle grows over every route point and label before scoring, so area and aspect price the canvas that ships.
