Phase 3, last step

Labels: measured, anchored, placed

A label is a box the application measured and requested; layout hangs it off its own route by a short invisible leader, inside the state that holds both ends, never over a foreign state or another route.

Measured by the application, with scav's metrics

The application measures, with scav's metrics: one implementation measures, builds and renders, so the three never disagree. A width in grid units is ceil_div(Σ advance × font size, units per em), summed in 64 bits and divided once. It rounds up, never to nearest, so a box is never smaller than its text. A box's height is the font size times a fixed ratio (269 units for 12 pt text on readable), not the font's own vertical metrics, which disagree with each other by 10 to 20%. Kerning is ignored, which errs wide for Latin text; a missing glyph is an error; there is one bundled font. The wrap width is always an input: layout never re-wraps a label, so height, width and placement need no fixpoint.

The request is a PathBox{subject, w, h, order}. Its size is an input: phase 1 charges its width to the rank gap its segment spans, and phase 2 reserves room beside the leg it will hang from. Its position is an output of phase 3, decided after every route is final. Both belong to the transition's LCA segment, the piece of the route in the innermost submachine holding both ends.

The anchored model

The box hangs off a point on its own polyline by an invisible leader of fixed length, half an em: max(font size / 2, 1), which is 96 units (6 pt) on readable. The leader runs up, down, left or right, so its length is exact in integers, and ends on one of eight attachment points of the box: bottom-mid, top-mid, right-mid, left-mid, then the four corners. Side midpoints come first so that a tie prefers a box centred over its leg; corners, which a bend needs, come last. The candidates are:

  • every axis-aligned leg of the route that lies in the LCA frame (legs inside the states the route leaves or enters by are dropped, and diagonal legs skipped),
  • × every slide position along that leg, one every max(h / 2, 1) units, with both ends included,
  • × the 4 leader directions × the 8 attachment points. The box's origin is the slide point plus the leader minus the attachment point.

Because the leader may run along the leg, a box can sit flush against its own leg, anywhere from 0 to one leader away.

Rules, in two tiersBoth tiers: the box lies inside its holder, the chart intersected with every state that encloses both ends (and with the region, when the LCA is one region of a concurrent state); no leg of its own route cuts it; and it does not overlap the two states its route leaves and enters that frame by. The strict tier adds: no overlap with any wall (a live state that does not enclose both ends, endpoints included; the enclosing states' text bands; boxes already placed), nor with another transition's legs. Touching is not overlapping.

Among the candidates that pass, the winner is the lexicographic minimum of (shortfall, dist, leg, attach, lead, slide):

  • shortfall = max(0, gap to its own leg + h − gap to the nearest foreign leg), with gaps measured as Chebyshev distance. A box closer to a neighbour's line than to its own reads as the neighbour's label, so it crosses to the far side of its own leg instead.
  • dist = the Manhattan distance from the box's centre to the anchor: the midpoint of the route's longest horizontal leg, or of its longest leg if none is horizontal.

Fallback. If no candidate passes the strict tier, the best loose one wins: anchored, inside its holder, uncut by its own route, but allowed over a state, a band, a placed box or a foreign route. If nothing passes at all, or the route is missing, the box is centred on the anchor and counted as unplaced. A transition with several boxes places them in order, each on legs at or after the previous one, further along the route.

Labels in the cost

Two label checks are Tier 0, forbidden rather than priced: a placed label over a foreign state (label_over_box) or over another transition's route (label_over_route). When one endpoint encloses the other, as in On -> On/Idle, the label lives inside that endpoint, so only its text bands count against it; an ordinary endpoint is not exempt. A label over another label, or over the text bands of the states it lives in, is Tier 2 (label), and the shortfall is Tier 2 as label_near.

Rejected along the wayBoxes on fixed strips at 0 to 4 box heights from the line, which sat axis's twist release seven text heights from its leg; rip-up-and-reroute for unplaceable labels, no longer needed since the loose tier; and holding "nothing between a label and its line" as a hard rule, which took fallbacks from 5 to 43 for no gain. (Label dummy nodes are rejected in ordering.) Pruning must use the strict tier's bound: pruning by the loose one pushed boxes into the fallback, 86 corpus defects against 60.
