Phase 3, last step

Labels: measured, anchored, placed

A label is a box the application measured; layout hangs it off its own route, inside the state holding both ends, clear of everything else.

Measured with scav's metrics

The application measures text with scav's own metrics, so measuring, building and rendering never disagree.

  • Widths are summed in integers and rounded up, never to nearest, so a box is never smaller than its text.
  • Heights are the font size times a fixed ratio (269 units for 12 pt), not the font's own vertical metrics, which disagree with each other.
  • Kerning is ignored (erring wide), a missing glyph is an error, and there is one bundled font.
  • Layout never re-wraps a label, so height, width and placement need no fixpoint.

A label's size is an input to ordering and sizing, which reserve room for it; its position is decided last, once every route is final. Both belong to the transition's LCA segment.

The anchored model

The box hangs off a point on its own route by an invisible leader half an em long (96 units). Candidates are every combination of:

  • a leg of the route inside the LCA frame,
  • a slide position along it, every half box height, ends included,
  • a leader direction (up, down, left, right),
  • one of eight attachment points on the box: side midpoints first (so ties centre the box on its leg), then corners.
RulesEvery candidate lies inside its holder (the states enclosing both ends), is not cut by its own route, and avoids the states its route leaves and enters. The strict tier also avoids every other state, text band, placed box and route; touching is not overlapping.

The winner is the lexicographic minimum of (shortfall, dist, leg, attach, lead, slide):

  • shortfall, max(0, gap to own leg + h − gap to nearest foreign leg): a box nearer a neighbour's line than its own would read as the neighbour's label.
  • dist: distance from the box's centre to the anchor, the midpoint of the route's longest horizontal leg (else its longest leg).

If no candidate passes the strict tier, the best loose one wins: it keeps the basic rules but may overlap. If nothing passes at all, the box is centred on the anchor and counted as unplaced. Several boxes on one transition are placed in order along the route. Candidates are pruned by the strict tier's bound; pruning by the loose one pushed boxes into the fallback.

Labels in the cost

A label over a foreign state or another route is Tier 0, forbidden rather than priced. If one endpoint encloses the other, the label lives inside it and only its text bands count. A label over another label or an enclosing state's bands is Tier 2 (label), as is the shortfall (label_near).

RejectedBoxes on fixed strips beside the line (they drifted far from their leg); rip-up-and-reroute (the loose tier made it unnecessary); "nothing between a label and its line" as a hard rule (many more fallbacks, no gain).
